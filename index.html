ABOUT NATUREX

A project built for
nature.

NatureX is an environmental project focused on protecting and restoring nature. Its vision is to encourage people to value trees, protect natural environments and participate in meaningful environmental action.

NatureX is being developed as a platform that combines environmental awareness, technology, sustainable products and future initiatives to inspire people to contribute to a greener world.

OUR MISSION

Restore. Protect.
Inspire.

Our mission is to turn environmental awareness into meaningful action.

01

Protect Nature

Encourage people and communities to value and protect trees, plants and natural environments.

02

Conserve Trees

Promote tree conservation and explore better solutions such as tree translocation where appropriate.

03

Inspire Action

Encourage people to move beyond awareness and take positive action for the environment.

FOUNDER & CEO

Akhil Karthikeya

Akhil Karthikeya is the Founder and CEO of NatureX. He is a Class 10 student at Sadhana Infinity International School and is designing and developing NatureX as an environmental project focused on nature conservation, environmental awareness and technology.

Through NatureX, Akhil aims to encourage people to protect nature, conserve trees, learn about native plants and explore technology-based ways of increasing environmental awareness.
