# NatureX 🌱

## Restore. Protect. Inspire.

NatureX is an environmental project focused on protecting nature, conserving trees, promoting native plants, raising environmental awareness, and encouraging meaningful action for a greener future.

NatureX is also exploring how technology and AI can support environmental learning, including plant identification and nature education.

### 🌳 What is NatureX?

NatureX aims to encourage people to:

- Protect trees and natural environments
- Support tree conservation and responsible tree translocation
- Learn about native plants
- Increase environmental awareness
- Use technology to learn more about nature
- Support sustainable and eco-friendly initiatives

### 🚀 NatureX Projects

- **Tree Conservation**
- **Native Plants**
- **NatureX AI**
- **AI Plant Scanner**
- **NatureX Store**
- **Environmental Awareness**

### 👤 Founder & CEO

**Akhil Karthikeya** is the Founder and CEO of NatureX.

NatureX is being designed and developed by Akhil Karthikeya, a Class 10 student at Sadhana Infinity International School, with the goal of building a project that combines environmental awareness, technology, and action for nature.

### 🌍 Vision

NatureX's vision is to inspire people to value nature and take meaningful steps toward a greener future.
