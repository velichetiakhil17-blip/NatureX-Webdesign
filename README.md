# NatureX 🌱

## Restore. Protect. Inspire.

**NatureX is an environmental project founded by Akhil Karthikeya.**

NatureX focuses on protecting nature, conserving trees, promoting native plants, raising environmental awareness, and encouraging meaningful action for a greener future.

NatureX is also exploring how technology and AI can support environmental learning, including plant identification and nature education.

---

## 🌳 What is NatureX?

NatureX aims to encourage people to:

- Protect trees and natural environments
- Support tree conservation and responsible tree translocation
- Learn about native plants
- Increase environmental awareness
- Use technology to learn more about nature
- Support sustainable and eco-friendly initiatives

## 🌱 Mission

The NatureX mission is to:

1. **Protect Nature**  
   Encourage people and communities to value and protect trees, plants, and natural environments.

2. **Conserve Trees**  
   Promote tree conservation and explore responsible solutions such as tree translocation where appropriate.

3. **Inspire Action**  
   Encourage people to move beyond awareness and take positive action for the environment.
